# Game brief

## Confirmed direction

SimpleEmpire is a hex-based, turn-based wargame of global conquest. Development
supports collaboration between its owner and different coding assistants.
The river, road, railroad, and bridge SVGs are visual experiments, not yet
approved production art or implemented movement rules.

## Proposed first playable milestone

A small local scenario with two sides: display a hex map, select a unit, show
legal movement, move it, end a turn, and resolve a simple conflict. The scenario
should have an explicit victory condition. This is a proposed scope to refine
before implementation, not a claim that these mechanics have been selected.

## Open decisions

- Platform, language, engine, and rendering approach.
- Map size, coordinate convention, terrain, and wrapping.
- Turn order, phases, action points, and movement costs.
- Unit model, stacking, combat, supply, and victory conditions.
- Hidden information, computer opponents, and multiplayer scope.
- Persistence, scenario format, and reproducibility requirements.
